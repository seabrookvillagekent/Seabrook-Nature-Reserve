SNR Leisure

Seabrook offers a mix of coast, canal, countryside and village amenities, making it easy to walk, paddle, explore and unwind. From the Royal Military Canal and the King Charles III England Coast Path to the Kent Downs, seafront refreshment stops and small local attractions, the area provides plenty of ways to enjoy the landscape at a gentle pace.
